NAJASHI MOBILE APP DEVELOPMENT — WEBSITE FILES

An shirya maka website na farko mai shafuka biyu:
- index.html: Babban shafin kasuwanci
- privacy.html: Privacy Policy na website
- styles.css: Tsarin launuka da responsive design
- script.js: Ƙaramin JavaScript na shekarar copyright

ABUBUWAN DA DOLE KA GYARA KAFIN KA WALLAFA:
1. Buɗe index.html da privacy.html.
2. Sauya duk inda aka rubuta REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-EMAIL da ainihin email ɗin kasuwancinka.
3. Tabbatar bayanin ayyukan da ke shafin ya dace da ainihin aikinka.
4. Idan kana da apps da aka wallafa, ƙara sunayensu da official Google Play links. Kada ka ƙara app ko link na ƙarya.
5. Ka karanta Privacy Policy ka gyara ta bisa ainihin yadda website ɗinka ke aiki. Wannan samfurin farko ne, ba shawarar doka ba.

YADDA ZA KA WALLAFA:
- Za ka iya amfani da Google Sites ta hanyar sake gina shafukan daga rubutun nan; Google Sites ba ya karɓar waɗannan HTML files kai tsaye a matsayin import.
- Idan kana son amfani da waɗannan HTML files kai tsaye, ka ɗora su a hosting mai tallafa wa static websites, misali GitHub Pages ko wani hosting naka.
- Bayan wallafawa, buɗe website ɗin a incognito/private browser domin tabbatar da cewa jama'a za su iya ganinsa ba tare da login ba.

MUHIMMI:
Wannan ZIP ɗin website files ne, ba ainihin live website ba tukuna. Ba a sayi domain ko ƙirƙirar hosting ba, kuma ba a aika komai zuwa Transsion ba.
